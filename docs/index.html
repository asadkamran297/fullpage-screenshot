What it does

  • One click, whole page. Click the toolbar icon or press Alt+Shift+S. The extension scrolls the page for you, captures every section, and stitches them into one sharp image.
  • Six export formats, none paywalled. Right-click the result and save as PNG, JPG, JPEG, WebP, lossless WebP, or a multi-page PDF. No watermark, no pro tier.
  • Copied to your clipboard automatically. Every capture lands on your clipboard the moment it is ready — paste it straight into Slack, Docs, Gmail or a bug report.
  • Completely private. Zero network requests and no host permissions. There is no server to upload to.
  • Sharp on HiDPI screens. Captures at your display's real pixel density, so text stays crisp on Retina and 4K displays.
  • No repeated headers. Sticky navigation bars and floating cookie banners are hidden after the first section, so they appear once instead of marching down the image.
  • Open source. MIT licensed, source on GitHub. Read exactly what it does before you trust it with your screen.

How to use it

  1. Open any page you want to capture.
  2. Click the Full Page Screenshot icon, or press Alt+Shift+S.
  3. Watch the progress indicator while the page scrolls and captures.
  4. The screenshot opens in a new tab and is already on your clipboard.
  5. Right-click the image to save it as PNG, JPG, WebP or PDF.

Your screenshots never leave your computer

Full Page Screenshot makes zero network requests. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no telemetry and no cloud storage. The capture is assembled in your browser and handed to a local tab. It requests no host permissions, which means it cannot read any page until you click its icon on that page.

Frequently asked questions

Does it capture the whole scrolling page?

Yes. Full Page Screenshot scrolls the entire page and stitches every section into a single image — the full height, not just the visible viewport.

Can I save a webpage as a PDF?

Yes. Right-click the captured image and choose PDF. Long pages are split across A4 pages automatically, and the PDF is generated inside the browser.

Is Full Page Screenshot free?

Completely. Every format and every feature is free. There is no paid tier, no watermark and no upsell.

Do I need an account to use it?

No. There is no sign-up, no login and no cloud service of any kind.

Are my screenshots uploaded anywhere?

Never. The extension makes zero network requests. Every capture happens inside your browser and the image never leaves your computer.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Nothing about the capture needs an internet connection.

Why can't it capture some pages?

Chrome blocks all extensions on browser-internal pages — chrome:// URLs, the Chrome Web Store and the extensions page. That is a browser rule, not a limitation of this extension.

What permissions does it request?

activeTab to read the pixels of the tab you clicked the icon on, scripting to inject the helper that measures and scrolls the page, storage to hand the finished image to the viewer tab, and clipboardWrite to copy the screenshot. It requests no host permissions, so it cannot read any site until you click its icon there.

Is Full Page Screenshot open source?

Yes. It is MIT licensed and the full source is on GitHub.

What are its limitations?

It has no annotation or editing tools, it cannot yet capture a scrollable panel inside a page or content inside an iframe, and it has no region or delayed capture. Very tall pages take a few seconds because Chrome limits how often a tab can be captured, and content that only loads on scroll can occasionally come out blank.
